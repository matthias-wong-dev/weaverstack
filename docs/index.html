Making Fabric easier to use, and safer to scale.

Weaver brings a code-first approach to native Fabric Lakehouses, Warehouses, Power BI models and Reports.

Keep working in Python, SQL and Power BI. Weaver handles the loading, dependencies, testing and deployment around your work, without another platform or service to host.

Free and open source. Uses your existing Fabric workspaces, compute and permissions.
